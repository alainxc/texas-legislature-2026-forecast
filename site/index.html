Who will control the Texas Legislature after November 3, 2026?

A FiveThirtyEight-style probabilistic forecast of all 150 Texas House seats and the 16 Texas Senate seats on the ballot, updated through 2026-11-01. Republicans hold both chambers going in.

The statewide environment

The model expects a generic Texas legislative race to run about R+4.2 statewide, a swing of D+9.4 from the 2024 presidential result (R+13.7). It blends 359 public polls of the governor race, the U.S. Senate race, Texas generic-ballot questions, the national generic ballot and Abbott approval with a structural midterm prior.
