2026-11-01 · Initial release

  • First public forecast of the 2026 Texas House (150 seats) and Texas Senate (16 seats up) elections.
  • Four-layer model: district baseline on current lines (TLC Report 206), candidate layer (incumbency, finance, prior performance, explicit third-party modelling), statewide environment (polls plus midterm prior with a Texas polling-bias correction), capped outside ratings and district polls.
  • 40,000-run correlated Monte Carlo simulation; backtests on 2022 and 2024.

Built from public data only: Texas Legislative Council, Texas Secretary of State, Texas Ethics Commission, Ballotpedia, and publicly released polls and ratings. Not affiliated with any campaign, caucus, party committee, or news outlet. Forecasts are probabilities, not predictions; see the methodology page for every assumption.
