# NOTES - Texas legislative general-election results (leg_results)

Generated by pipeline/fetch/fetch_leg_results.py on 2026-11-01T21:20. Nothing hand-typed.

## Sources (all official Texas Secretary of State)

- 2020, 2022, 2024: SOS results portal results.texas-election.com, static JSON `static/data/election/<id>/<version>/Districted.json` (ids 44144 / 47009 / 49664). Same data as the portal's District Offices tab / CSV download. Candidate names carry an `(I)` incumbent marker, which is parsed into `incumbent`.
- 2018: SOS historical results `elections.sos.state.tx.us/elchist331_state.htm` (2018 General Election Race Summary Report, canvass votes, `(I)` marker).
- 2022 unopposed seats: SOS qualified-candidate list (candidate.texas-election.com, 2022 NOVEMBER 8TH GENERAL ELECTION, STATE offices) for name / party / incumbent.
- SOS portal CSV downloads are produced client-side from these JSON files; the JSON is the underlying data so it is what we archive.

## Important caveat: 2022 unopposed races were not on the ballot

In the 2022 general election, Texas legislative races with a single qualified candidate were cancelled and the candidate was declared elected without appearing on the ballot. Consequently neither the SOS results portal (92 House / 21 Senate races only) nor the Texas Legislative Council VTD returns contain any votes for them, and Ballotpedia district pages say the general election 'was canceled'. For those districts `leg_results.csv` has one row from the SOS qualified-candidate list with blank votes/pct, `winner=True`; `leg_races.csv` has `uncontested=True`, `on_ballot=False`, blank pcts/margin, winner_party and incumbent party set. In 2018/2020/2024 unopposed candidates did appear on the ballot and have votes (pct 100).

## Column notes

- `pct` is recomputed as votes / race total * 100 (2 decimals); matches SOS to rounding.
- `uncontested` = at most one non-write-in candidate. `other_pct` = sum of non-D/R candidates including write-ins. `margin_d_minus_r` = dem_pct - rep_pct in points (2 decimals).
- `incumbent` is True/False from the SOS `(I)` marker (2018-2024 results), the SOS candidate list INCUMBENT field (2022 unopposed) or Ballotpedia's 'Incumbent X won' sentence (2022 fallback). It is as published: a spot check found at least one incumbent without the SOS marker (HD1 2018, Gary VanDeaver, unopposed), so False means 'not marked incumbent by the source'.
- `incumbent_party_if_known` is blank when no candidate in the race was marked incumbent (open seat / new district).
- Party codes: R, D, L (Libertarian), G (Green), I (Independent), W (write-in).
- Senate: only the seats up in each year are present (all 31 in 2022 after redistricting; roughly half otherwise). 2020/2024 use PLANS2168 only for 2022+; 2018/2020 use old lines (PLANH2100/PLANS2100-era maps), as expected for the back-test years.

## Gaps / alternatives

- No gaps: 150 House rows per year for 2018, 2020, 2022, 2024; all contested Senate seats present.
- Ballotpedia (the designated fallback) was used only for the 2022 seat(s) listed above; raw HTML saved in raw/.
- Texas Legislative Council open data (data.capitol.texas.gov 'Comprehensive Election Datasets', VTD-level returns with an Incumbent column) was inspected as a cross-check; it has the same 92 House races for 2022 and could be used to rebuild district totals from VTDs if ever needed.
